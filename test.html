About me

I am a New York City-based photographer specializing in portrait photography, dedicated to capturing the essence and personality of every subject. My work combines technical expertise with a keen eye for detail, creating images that are both striking and authentic. Inspired by the vibrant energy of the city, I strive to turn every portrait into a story, reflecting the individuality and emotion of the person in front of the lens.

Passionate about collaboration and creative expression, I work closely with clients to bring their vision to life. From editorial shoots to personal portraits, my goal is to craft images that resonate and leave a lasting impression. I aim to create portraits that go beyond a simple photograph — capturing the unique spirit of each individual with artistry and intention.
